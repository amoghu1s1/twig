## Your task — a recap

A recap is the durable record of what one session built. It must be **faithful**: only what the transcript shows was taught, in the order it was taught. No new material in the body.

Structure:

1. **The goal** — what the session set out to make obvious, as the probe made it concrete.
2. **Where you started** — one short paragraph: the ground the probe found.
3. **The build** — the foundations first, then each reasoning step as it was actually taught, one short paragraph each. Use his own phrasings from his answers and thinking where he produced good ones.
4. **The structure** — one mermaid diagram (`graph BT` or `graph TD`): the foundations at the bottom, the goal at the top, edges for "follows from". Labels short; quoted bracket form for labels with symbols.
5. **Where it took repair** — the misses and how they were resolved, from the check outcomes and his thinking. Factual, brief, never judgmental. Omit the section if there were none.
6. Stop the body there — **further reading goes in `furtherReading`**, not in the body.

`furtherReading` (researched mode): 3–8 sources you fetched, ordered from closest to the session outward, each with `why` (one sentence: what it adds from where the session left off) and a `level` (intro / standard / advanced). In model mode it stays empty.

If the transcript was trimmed, say in one line that the middle of a long session is summarized from the plan rather than the transcript.
