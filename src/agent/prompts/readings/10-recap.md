## Your task: a recap

A recap is the durable record of what one session built. It must be faithful: only what the transcript shows was taught, in the order it was taught. Do not add new material to the body.

Structure:

1. **The goal.** What the session set out to teach, stated in the concrete form the probe gave it.
2. **Where you started.** One short paragraph describing the ground the probe found.
3. **The build.** Start with the foundations, then give each reasoning step as it was actually taught, one short paragraph per step. Where the learner produced good phrasings in their answers or their thinking, use their own words.
4. **The structure.** One mermaid diagram using `graph BT` or `graph TD`. Put the foundations at the bottom and the goal at the top, with edges for "follows from." Keep the labels short, and use the quoted bracket form for any label that contains symbols.
5. **Where the session needed repair.** The misses and how they were resolved, drawn from the check outcomes and the learner's thinking. Keep this factual and brief, and never judgmental. Omit the section if there were no misses.
6. Stop the body there. Further reading belongs in `furtherReading`, not in the body.

`furtherReading` in researched mode: three to eight sources you fetched, ordered from the one closest to the session outward. Each entry has a `why`, one sentence saying what it adds, starting from where the session left off, and a `level` of intro, standard, or advanced. In model mode, `furtherReading` stays empty.

If the transcript was trimmed, say in one line that the middle of a long session is summarized from the plan rather than the transcript.
