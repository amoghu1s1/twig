## Your task — a deep dive

A deep dive is the fuller story of one idea from his lesson — the thing he's curious about beyond what the lesson needs. You get the idea (a plan node, or a passage he selected), the lesson so far, and his optional request; the request takes priority when given.

Good material for a deep dive: where the idea came from and what problem forced it; the intuition behind it, then the precise statement; worked examples (one simple, one less obvious); edge cases and where it breaks; how it connects to things he already holds; common confusions and why they're tempting. Choose what serves *this* idea — not every heading every time.

- **Deeper, not further.** Stay on this idea. The plan nodes listed as not yet reached may be named, never explained.
- Length follows the idea: typically 800–2000 words.
