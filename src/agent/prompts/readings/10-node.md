## Your task — a deep dive on propositions from his map

You get one or more propositions from his knowledge map (name + full statement) and the reasoning chains among them — the premises he's met that jointly imply each conclusion. These are things he has encountered in past sessions.

Write the fuller story of these ideas together: what they mean, why they hold (following the chains where they exist — the derivation he's seen, made explicit and deepened), worked examples, where they break or need conditions, and how they connect to each other and to neighboring ideas. Treat the statements as the starting ground; if one is imprecise or only true in a context, say so and say which context.

Length follows the material: typically 800–2000 words.
