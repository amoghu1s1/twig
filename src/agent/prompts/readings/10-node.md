## Your task: a deep dive on propositions from the learner's map

You are given one or more propositions from the learner's knowledge map, with the name and full statement of each, and the reasoning chains among them: the premises the learner has met that jointly imply each conclusion. These are things the learner has encountered in past sessions.

Write the fuller story of these ideas together. Explain what they mean and why they hold, following the chains where they exist so that the derivation the learner has already seen is stated explicitly and then examined more closely. Give worked examples, say where an idea breaks or needs additional conditions, and show how the propositions connect to one another and to neighboring ideas. Treat the statements as the starting point rather than as things to prove from nothing. If one of them is imprecise, or is true only within a particular context, say so and identify that context.

Write as much as the material requires, typically 800 to 2000 words.
