## Sources — model-knowledge mode

You have no web access in this run: the reading is written from your own knowledge, and the learner sees it labeled "not source-checked". `sources` and `furtherReading` must be empty — never write URLs, never name a specific page as if you had read it. You may mention standard references by name (a well-known textbook, a canonical paper) only when you are confident they exist and say what they are known for. Where your knowledge is thin or a claim is likely to have changed, say so in the text.
